## Repository Overview

This is a content repository, not an application. It contains a Chinese-language book, 《道德经81章投资心法》, and its source material. `python3 scripts/build_codex_epub.py` rebuilds and validates the Codex EPUB (Python 3 and Pillow required).

- `chapters/第01章.md` … `第81章.md` — the book: one essay per Tao Te Ching chapter (81 files, zero-padded names)
- `codex/第01章.md` … `第81章.md` — the rewritten Codex narrative edition: exact source text followed by a plain-language `投资心法` and a practical recommendation under 100 Chinese characters; `codex/README.md` is its methodology and index
- `道德经81章投资心法Codex版本.epub` — the generated EPUB 3 edition, including its cover, dual-format navigation, 81 chapters, and the Wang Bi source appendix
- `scripts/build_codex_epub.py` — the reproducible EPUB builder and structural validator
- `投资心法100条精选版.md` / `投资心法313条全量版.md` — the three-book distillation (this book, the Codex edition, and 第三只眼观): 313 per-chapter 心法 merged into 100 ranked entries, each carrying the verbatim source quotes with attributions. `python3 scripts/build_selection_epub.py` rebuilds and validates `投资心法100条精选版.epub` (9 themed parts, 100 entry pages, 400 quotes; the validator asserts entry numbering, per-part ranges, and quote counts against the Markdown source)
- `jinbing-drdj/第01篇.md` … `第72篇.md` — reading notes on 金冰《人生悟道 渡人渡己·投资篇（纪念版）》 (72 articles in three parts: 基础篇 1–25, 进阶篇 26–48, 哲思篇 49–72). Each file is `## 中心思想` (faithful précis of that article, anchored on its own examples) + `## 投资心法` (600–1500-char independently written essay) + `## 实操建议` (≤100 chars); `jinbing-drdj/README.md` is its index and methodology. `python3 scripts/build_jinbing_epub.py` rebuilds and validates `人生悟道渡人渡己投资篇读书心法.epub`. **The source book is a third-party work: it is gitignored (`jinbing-drdj/*.pdf`) and must not be committed.** Every verbatim source string must sit inside `<mark>` (≤2 per file, ≤30 chars each) and is verified character-by-character against the PDF at build time; any 30-character run shared with the source outside `<mark>` fails the build.
- `投资体系与方法论.md` / `投资体系与方法论·道德经对照版.md` — the operating-system edition: the five source books (this book, the Codex edition, 第三只眼观, 金冰 reading notes, and the 100-entry selection) exhaustively mined and reorganized into a runnable system — a decision loop, nine dimensions (认知/研究/风控/仓位/买卖点/周期/心力/纪律/修炼), twelve tension arbitrations, a calendar-based operations manual, and fourteen copyable forms. Every principle, rule, and number carries a source tag (`道N` / `道N章实操` / `CodexN` / `三眼N` / `金N篇实操` / `选N`); editor-supplied connective reasoning is marked `（综合）`; gaps the corpus does not cover are declared verbatim as 「语料未覆盖」. Machine-verified coverage: 道 81/81, Codex 81/81, 三眼 151/151, 金 72/72, all 234 实操建议, all 100 selection groups.
  - The two files differ **only** by one layer: the 对照版 adds 680 《道德经》 source-text annotations plus 附录D. Annotations use the `▍` marker (`▍《道德经》第二十四章：…` full form for 核心原则; `▍二十四章「企者不立」` compact form for rules) — **Chinese numerals after `▍` mean the 道德经 original chapter, Arabic numerals inside `[…]` mean a chapter of one of the five心法 books**. ## Content Conventions

Every original file in `chapters/` follows one fixed format — preserve it when editing:

- H1: `# 第X章 · 核心句` (Chinese-numeral chapter number + the chapter's most resonant line)
- `## 原文`: the full chapter as a blockquote, copied verbatim from `原文/道德经-王弼本.md` — simplified characters (base file is itself a t2s conversion; see its header for the conversion notes), including editorial brackets 〔〕 and small-form punctuation ﹖﹕﹗､, never paraphrased. Any 原文 change must first be made in the base file, with a header 校记 note.
- `## 投资心法`: a 600–1500-character simplified-Chinese essay in the established style: personal/life entry point → at least one concrete market scene (with sensory detail) → 先破后立 → circle back to the original lines → closing antithetical distillation (对仗式心法, e.g. "为学日益为向外求…／为道日损为向内修…"). Chapters on statecraft/war extend naturally into risk, cycles, or governance — never forced. Verbatim 道德经 quotes inside the essay are wrapped in `<mark>…</mark>` (styled bold + gold via `index.html` CSS); additionally, exactly two law-like/summarizing aphorisms per essay are wrapped in `<u>…</u>` (styled as a green underline). Preserve both kinds of tags when editing and apply them to any newly added quote or aphorism the same way. No per-file disclaimer (README carries the single disclaimer).
- `## 实操建议` (added 2026-08-19): one plain-prose paragraph of 1–3 imperative sentences, ≤100 non-whitespace characters, derived from the essay's own scenes and closing 心法 — concrete behavioral discipline (a trigger, number, or checkable rule), no `<mark>`/`<u>` tags, no lists, no investor quotes, no stock/sector tips. It is written fresh per chapter and intentionally does not duplicate the codex edition's 实操建议.

Codex files use `## 原文`, `## 投资心法`, and `## 实操建议`, followed by navigation. The prose standard is: an everyday or personal entry point, a concrete market scene, a plain-language explanation that returns to the original lines, and a memorable closing distillation. Each Codex essay must be independently written rather than adapted from `chapters/`: the EPUB builder compares it with all 81 main-book essays and rejects normalized similarity at or above 30% or reuse of a complete paragraph of at least 50 characters. Keep each advice paragraph below 100 characters and preserve the methodology and disclaimer in `codex/README.md`.

Cross-cutting standards (apply to chapters and the compass essay alike):

- **Attribution rigor**: investor quotes must trace to verified primary sources; uncertain ones are phrased as paraphrase ("芒格说过，大意是……"). Known corrections stay corrected — the "stock forecasters / fortune tellers" line is Buffett (1992 shareholder letter), not Graham; Livermore's "sitting, not thinking" is Lefèvre's literary rendering in *Reminiscences of a Stock Operator*.
- **Empirical claims cite sources with scope limits**: e.g. Barber & Odean (2000, *The Journal of Finance*), 66,465 accounts, 1991–1996 US discount-broker sample — not extrapolated to today's Chinese market.
- **No stock tips**: sectors and episodes (光模块、2015配资牛市 etc.) appear only as illustrations of behavior, never recommendations.
